Bet Track is the second doorway of Ivrit HaOr. It is designed for students who have begun recognizing Hebrew letters and are ready to start building Hebrew sounds, letter pairs, simple words, and early decoding habits.

Students in Bet Track still review Aleph Track skills. They remember that Hebrew begins on the right, identify familiar letters, and begin noticing how Hebrew letters work together to form words.

Bet Track Purpose

In Aleph Track, students learned to see Hebrew letters. In Bet Track, students begin learning how Hebrew letters help build sounds and words.

A Hebrew word is built one small step at a time.

  • Letter by letter.
  • Sound by sound.
  • Word by word.

Bet Track is where Hebrew letters begin working together.

Bet Track — Lesson 1

Building the Word

Letters Can Join Sounds

Big Idea

In Aleph Track, I learned to see Hebrew letters. In Bet Track, I begin learning how Hebrew letters help build sounds and words.

A Hebrew word is built one small step at a time.

  • Letter by letter.
  • Sound by sound.
  • Word by word.

Teacher says:

“Bet Track is where Hebrew letters begin working together.”

Student says:

“I can build Hebrew sounds and words.”

Look and Remember

Hebrew begins on the right.

Look at this Hebrew path:

RIGHT SIDE ← ← ← LEFT SIDE

When I read Hebrew, my eyes begin on the right.

Teacher says:

“Before we read a word, we find the starting side.”

Student says:

“Hebrew begins on the right.”

Letters We Know

Look at these Hebrew letters.

א ב ע מ ש ל ו י ה

Some may feel familiar. Some may still need practice. That is okay.

Review is not going backward. Review is strengthening the path.

Point and Say

Point to each letter. Say the letter name if you know it.

If you do not know it yet, say:

“I am still learning this one.”

Circle the Letters You Know

Circle the Hebrew letters you can recognize.

א ב ע מ ש ל ו י ה

Find Bet

Circle every Bet.

ב א ב ע ב מ

Find Aleph

Circle every Aleph.

א ב ע א מ א

Find Ayin

Circle every Ayin.

ע א ב ע ש ע

Teacher Question

What do Hebrew words begin with?

Confidence Check

Fill in one.

  • ○ I am still remembering Hebrew letters.

  • ○ I can begin on the right side.
  • ○ I am ready to build Hebrew words.

Build from the Right

Big Idea

Hebrew words are read from right to left. I can slow down and look carefully.

Teacher says:

“In Bet Track, we do not guess. We build.”

Student says:

“I can build from the right.”

Start on the Right

Put your finger on the first Hebrew letter.

Remember: Hebrew begins on the right.

Coming Next
