Welcome to Aleph

A 36-lesson first Hebrew sequence for right-to-left direction, letter recognition, vowel cues, familiar words, and confidence before Bet. The order groups useful visual and sound families instead of following strict alphabet order.

Use a steady rhythm: look carefully, listen to a model, practice together, then try independently. The printable workbook and online lessons follow this same sequence.
