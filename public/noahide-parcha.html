What Is Noahide Daily Parcha?

Noahide Daily Parcha is a flexible study plan for people of the nations who wish to learn from a short part of the weekly Torah portion. “Daily” means a small optional practice that can be spread across the week; it does not create a religious obligation or imitate Jewish prayer, synagogue, or Shabbat observance.

Torah belongs to Israel. The nations may learn with humility.

Seven-Law Reflection Prompts

These are broad prompts for reflection, not a checklist to attach to every passage. Names and formulations can differ among teachers; ask qualified rabbinic guidance about religious obligations or practical law.

  • Worship and reverence: What does the passage say about honoring the Creator?
  • Speech: Does it raise a question about speech toward God?
  • Life: How are people protected, harmed, or held responsible?
  • Family: What relationships or boundaries appear in the text?
  • Property: What does the passage say about taking, keeping, or restoring?
  • Justice: What fair process or responsibility is shown?
  • Living creatures: What care or restraint is modeled?

Sample Reflection: Bereishit — Beginning with the Creator

Portion: Bereishit

Hebrew Word: אוֹר / light

Source: Genesis 1:3

Commentary doorway: Rashi on Genesis 1:3

Noahide Lens: The world is not ownerless. Creation begins with the Creator.

Reflection: What responsibility follows from seeing the created world as entrusted rather than ownerless?

Practical Step: Name one area of life where a small act of order or care would help.

Question: What does this passage itself say, and what part is my application?
