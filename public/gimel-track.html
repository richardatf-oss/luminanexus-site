Hebrew words do more than make sounds. Hebrew words carry meaning.

Gimel Track is the third doorway of Ivrit HaOr. It is designed for students who are ready to move beyond letter recognition and early word-building into reading short Hebrew words with meaning.

Students in Gimel Track still review letters and direction, but the central goal is deeper: see the letters, read the word, and ask what the word means.

Gimel Track Purpose

In Gimel Track, students do more than recognize Hebrew letters. They begin reading Hebrew with meaning.

A Hebrew word is not only something to sound out. A Hebrew word can carry:

  • an idea
  • a memory
  • a doorway
  • a meaning

Gimel Track is where reading begins to open into meaning.
