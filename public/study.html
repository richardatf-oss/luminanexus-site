Three ways to begin

Read in context

Source Library

Guided reading cards connect Hebrew learning, biblical passages, Jewish context, and traditional commentary. Filter by topic and readiness.

Open the Source Library
A simple first session
  1. Choose one card and read the linked passage in context.
  2. Write down what the text says before opening commentary.
  3. Keep one question for your next study session.

Letters & contemplation

The 231 Gates

Explore the pairings of the 22 Hebrew letters through Sefer Yetzirah, source notes, and mystical reflections. Compare lenses and save a gate to revisit.

Explore the gates
A simple first session
  1. Choose a two-letter gate and identify each letter.
  2. Read its source notes and compare the available interpretations.
  3. Keep your own reflection distinct from a traditional teaching.

A gentle daily rhythm

Noahide Daily Parcha

An optional seven-day path through a short passage from the weekly Torah portion, with source links and a personal reflection journal.

Open the daily study path
A simple first session
  1. Find the portion using the calendar or enter the one you are studying.
  2. Read a short passage and notice one word or question.
  3. Record the text, commentary, and personal reflection separately.

Learn together

A study partner makes room for another question.

Take turns reading a short passage. Each partner shares an observation before offering an interpretation. Return to the text together.
