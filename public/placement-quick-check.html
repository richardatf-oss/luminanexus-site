Placement Quick-Check

The purpose of placement is not to rank students. The purpose is to find the right doorway.

Teacher Instructions

  • Observe gently.
  • Do not call it a test.
  • Tell students: “We are finding your starting doorway.”
  • Students may move tracks later.

Three Doorways

Aleph Doorway

A student may begin here if they:

  • do not know Hebrew begins on the right
  • recognize few or no Hebrew letters
  • need help telling Hebrew and English letters apart
  • need tracing and visual confidence
  • need Aleph, Ayin, Bet, and Vet recognition practice

Bet Doorway

A student may begin here if they:

  • know several Hebrew letters
  • remember right-to-left direction
  • can identify Aleph, Bet, Ayin, and perhaps Mem, Shin, Lamed, Vav, Yod, or Hey
  • are ready to build letter pairs and simple syllables
  • can begin working toward words like שלום

Gimel Doorway

A student may begin here if they:

  • recognize many letters
  • can read or attempt short Hebrew words
  • can work with אור
  • are ready to connect words with meaning
  • can notice roots or word families with support

Quick Student Prompts

  1. Point to where Hebrew begins.
  2. Circle the Hebrew letters: A א B ב C ע
  3. Can you find Aleph? א ב ע א
  4. Can you find Bet? ב א ב ע
  5. Look at שלום. Which side do your eyes start on?
  6. Look at אור. Can you say it or tell what it means?

Placement Note

A student may belong between tracks. That is normal. Begin where confidence can grow.

The tracks are doorways, not labels. The student is not a score. The beginning is not a weakness.
