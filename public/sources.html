Source Library Purpose

The Source Library gives teachers and advanced learners a respectful doorway into Hebrew source texts. These links are for language, literacy, context, and cultural understanding. They do not replace a rabbi, Jewish educator, or qualified authority.

Sefaria.org

Sefaria.org is a digital library of Jewish texts, translations, and commentaries. LuminaNexus links to Sefaria as an external reference, not as a substitute for guided Jewish learning.

Classroom Boundary Note

These links support Hebrew literacy, cultural context, and teacher reference. Ivrit HaOr is not a halachic authority, does not provide conversion guidance, and does not replace a rabbi, Jewish educator, or Jewish communal learning. Teachers should keep source use age-appropriate and school-safe.
